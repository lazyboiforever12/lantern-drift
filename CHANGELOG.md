# Changelog

Patch notes for Lantern Drift. Play it at https://lazyboiforever12.github.io/lantern-drift/ or on https://lazyboi-forever.itch.io/lantern-drift

## 1.1 (12 November 2026): Act II, The Green Light

### New
- The story doubles to 18 levels. Act II (levels 10 to 18) follows a mysterious green light out to sea, with a story beat after every level and a new ending.
- Floodgates: walls that sink once you light half the buoys, opening new routes mid-level. In story levels, Endless, the Daily Voyage and the level editor (new Floodgate tool).
- Eclipses: every so often the moon turns blood red and goes dark. Your lantern shrinks and shadows get bolder until it passes. In story levels, Endless, the Daily Voyage and the editor (new Eclipse twist).
- The level list is split into two acts. Shift + 1 to 9 picks act II levels.
- Three new achievements: Lock Keeper, Blood Moon and The Last Keeper.
- Share previews: links to the game now show a picture and description on Discord, X and other apps.

### Fixed
- The game now pauses and goes quiet when you switch tabs or minimise the window.
- If the graphics driver restarts (laptops can do this after sleep), the game reloads itself instead of showing a black screen. Progress is kept.
- Achievement pop-ups no longer cover the timer and buoy counter.
- Long tips no longer run underneath the minimap.
- The bot that sails the title-screen demo could get stuck against a lighthouse.
- Endless: floodgate voyages start at voyage 9, so they don't pile onto the first brute.
- Level names in share links can no longer contain HTML.
- Faster loading: the browser now downloads all game files at once.

Saves carry over: if you had beaten Lighthouse Point, act II is already unlocked.

## 1.0 (November 2026)

### New
- Ghost boats: race a pale replay of your best run on story levels and the Daily Voyage.
- The level list shows how many ember shards you have found on each level.
- Pause menu with buttons, and tappable "back" lines on result screens, for phone players.
- Gamepad: the d-pad picks blessings, X restarts and Select quits from the pause menu.
- Water detail: wind ripples, raindrop rings, and floating things that ride the waves.
- Two new story levels: The Frozen Quay and Whirlpool Reach (9 in total).
- Lifetime stats on the achievements screen.
- First-sight tips for whirlpools, wisps, brutes, shards and the dash.
- Minimap showing walls, currents, buoys, shards, the lighthouse and your boat.
- Wisps and brutes: two new kinds of shadow.
- Endless mode blessings: choose one of three run upgrades after each voyage.
- Weather: rain, snow, fog and clear nights, each with its own ambience.
- Dash (Shift): a short burst past top speed for a little oil.
- Night sky with moon, stars and drifting clouds, reflected in the water.
- Photo mode (C) with a free orbit camera, and sharing results from Daily and Endless.
- Live title screen: the boat sails the levels behind the menu.
- Whirlpools and ember shards.
- Wall foam and ambient occlusion.
- 23 achievements with Embers rewards.
- Town detail: roofs, chimneys, framed windows, piers, moored boats and lamp posts.
- Level editor v2: box and fill tools, mirror, undo, level settings in share links, random maps.
- Ray-traced shadows, volumetric light, resolution options up to 4K, and 60/120/240/uncapped frame rates.
- Daily Voyage and Endless mode.
- The Boatyard: 11 boats with traits and animated parts, upgrades and paints.

### Fixed
- Glowing outlines along walls in the dark.
- After an update, a browser could mix old and new game files and fail to load. Each file now carries a version tag.
- 4K could fail to start on some graphics chips; it now uses fewer samples and falls back safely.
- The save could lose the Boatyard on reload.
- Developer tools no longer load in the public game.
